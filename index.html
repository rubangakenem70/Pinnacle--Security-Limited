❓ Frequently Asked Questions - Click To Expand

How much does a guard cost per month?

Price depends on location, day/night, armed/unarmed, and number of guards. A single day guard in Kampala is affordable and we give a written quote after a FREE site visit. Fill the Free Quote form above and we will call you with exact price - no hidden charges.

Are your guards trained and vetted?

Yes. Every guard is police-vetted, has a valid NIN, is trained in guarding, customer care, first aid, fire safety and emergency response. Guards are supervised daily and replaced immediately if sick or absent.

Do you cover my area? I am in Wakiso / Entebbe / upcountry?

We cover Muyenga, Bukasa, Makindye, Kansanga, Ggaba, Munyonyo, Kabalagala, Kampala CBD, Wakiso, Entebbe and surrounding areas. For upcountry contracts (Mbarara, Jinja, Gulu, etc.), call us - we arrange a team.

What happens if my alarm rings at night?

Our 24/7 control room receives the alert, calls you, and sends a response team to your site in minutes. We check the compound, secure it, and call police if needed. You get a full report the next morning.

Can I cancel or change my guard anytime?

Yes. We work on monthly contracts. You can change day/night, add guards, or cancel with notice. If you are not happy with a guard, we replace him FREE - same day.

Do you install CCTV? Can I watch from my phone?

Yes. We install HD CCTV with night vision and remote viewing. You can watch your home/shop live from your phone anywhere in the world. We also do alarms, electric fence and gate automation.

How do I pay? Do you accept Mobile Money?

Yes. We accept MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, bank transfer and cash. You get a receipt and monthly invoice. Payment is monthly in advance.

How fast can you start? I need a guard tomorrow.

Call us now on 0785 557 6219. For urgent cases, we can deploy a guard within 24 hours after a quick site check. For events, book 2-3 days before.

Do you offer jobs? How do I apply?

Yes. We are always hiring disciplined guards, supervisors and CCTV operators. Fill the Apply For Job form above with your NIN and district. We will call you for interview and training.

What if I want to report theft or suspicious person?

If it is an emergency, call us on 0785 557 6219 or Police 999 first. Then fill the Report An Incident form. Keep details, photos and time - it helps police and our team.
